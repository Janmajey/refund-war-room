From 26 October, Tier-3 pincodes served from Hosur and Hyderabad moved from SwiftShip to RapidKart to absorb Diwali volume. RapidKart is loading parcels on open trucks for the last mile and we are already seeing more damaged parcels. We have asked RapidKart for a fix by 4 November.
